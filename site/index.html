Everything a track can tell you

Open a file and get a map, a background estimate and a verdict, with the uncertainty spelled out.

Map every reading

Colour your walk, drive or flight by dose rate on a map, and select any stretch to see what it measured.

Honest statistics

When nothing stands out, it tells you the smallest signal it could have seen, instead of a bare "nothing found".

Hotspots you can trust

Spots that stay elevated on more than one pass are told apart from one-off flickers of counting noise.

Clean GPS

Phone-grade GPS jitter is smoothed away, so distances and speeds on foot come out right.

Walks to flights

Recorded on a plane? Bring in the flight's altitude and watch cosmic radiation climb with height.

Export anything

Figures as PNG or SVG, tracks as GPX, tables as CSV, and a full report as JSON.

Three steps

No account and no cloud: it runs on your own computer.

Open

Drop in the .rctrk file from the RadiaCode app.

Explore

Pan the map, read the series, check the hotspots and the background.

Share

Export a figure or a report with its numbers and provenance attached.

Up in the air, too

On a flight, dose rate is driven by altitude, which the track doesn't record. The app fetches the flight's path, checks it really matches your track, and fits how radiation rises with height, keeping climb and descent separate.
